TITLE

House on a Pebble


TAGLINE

A tiny housemate lives on your wrist, cooking, reading and stargazing as the day goes by.


DESCRIPTION

Your watch now has a tenant. A little character in a bobble hat lives in a cross-section house above the time and follows a daily routine: a bath in the morning, cooking at mealtimes, tinkering with a model rocket in the attic, TV on the sofa, a look through the telescope after dark, and bed at eleven.

Every so often your housemate walks to another room, up and down the stairs. Sky and house follow the time of day, from a pink dawn to a starry night, when only the room your housemate is in stays lit. The lights go out at bedtime. Flick your wrist to see what is going on: the current activity plays for a few seconds.

The animation only runs for a few seconds at a time, so the face stays still and easy on the battery the rest of the time. Made for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2, with the house drawn to fit each screen.


SETTINGS

- Moves to another room - every minute, every 5 minutes, every 15 minutes or every hour. More frequent walks use a little more battery. At night your housemate stays in bed whatever you choose.
- Vibrate before walking - off by default. A short buzz three seconds before your housemate sets off, so you can watch. Stays silent during Quiet Time, at bedtime and when getting up.

12 or 24 hour format follows the watch setting.
